How It Works

Multilevel Queue Scheduling divides the ready queue into separate queues based on process type or priority. A process is permanently assigned to one queue. Here, Queue 1 has higher priority than Queue 2. Each queue can use its own scheduling algorithm.

  1. Processes arrive and enter their assigned queue.
  2. The highest-priority non-empty queue is selected.
  3. The selected queue applies its configured algorithm.
  4. Completion and waiting/turnaround metrics are calculated.
