How Round Robin Works

  1. Processes enter the ready queue according to Arrival Time.
  2. The first process gets the CPU for at most one Time Quantum.
  3. If it is unfinished, it goes to the back of the ready queue.
  4. Newly arrived processes join the ready queue in arrival order.
  5. Continue until every process completes.
